Broadcast Engineering Society (India) · Since 1987

A professional society.
A shared purpose.

BES India is a community for people who work in broadcast engineering. Established in New Delhi in 1987, the Society supports the exchange of technical knowledge through conferences, seminars, tutorials, and exhibitions.

Our work continues beyond the annual EXPO. Through local chapters, the BES Review technical journal, and opportunities for professional exchange, members stay connected to the practice and development of broadcasting.
